**The solution in one paragraph.** Each region ("cell") is generated by PCG's own runtime scheduler, with its props spawned **invisible to navigation** but with collision on. When a cell finishes generating, NavReady **commits** it to navigation (one cell per frame by default), which makes navigation rebuild that cell's tiles exactly once, from complete content. NavReady then watches **that cell's** tiles until none are pending or running, hashes the cell's generated content, and only then fires the cell's **AI-ready** event. Agents assigned to the cell stay dormant until that event.

## E. Technical demonstration

**What the viewer sees**
- A flat arena seen from above, with a camera flying a fixed path over a 4 × 4 grid of cells.
- As the camera approaches, each cell fills with rocks (cubes and cylinders).
- The navmesh is drawn: green where it is valid, missing where tiles are being rebuilt.
- Each cell's border is tinted by its pipeline state: grey (empty), blue (generating), yellow (committed, navigation catching up), green (AI-ready).
- Small agents spawn in each cell. Dormant agents are dim; active agents move. A path that crosses a rock flashes red.
- An overlay shows the configuration (B0–B3 or NR), frame and game-thread time, and live counters: path failures, cells ready.

**Before** (B0, then B1–B3)
- **B0:** agents start moving the moment a cell appears. Some walk straight through rocks the navmesh doesn't know about yet (red flashes).
- **B1:** whenever generation pauses, the whole navmesh disappears and rebuilds; agents everywhere stop or fail, and the frame graph spikes.
- **B2:** better, but while any cell is generating, no other cell's navmesh updates.
- **B3:** agents wait for "navigation idle", which during the fast burst phase arrives late, for every cell at once.

**After** (NR)
- Each cell turns green on its own schedule, shortly after its rocks appear.
- No red flashes: agents only move once their cell's navmesh is rebuilt.
- The frame graph shows no full-rebuild spike.
